# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Registered private users who track personal income, expenses, budgets, and cashflow through Telegram. They primarily use the product in Indonesian on a mobile Telegram Mini App, often after recording transactions quickly through chat. Their job is to understand where money went, keep budgets under control, and ask for practical cashflow guidance without feeling like they are using a complex finance platform.

## Product Purpose

The product is a private Indonesian personal finance tracker combining Telegram chat input, a Mini App dashboard, budgeting tools, transaction management, settings, and an AI advisor. Success means users can record transactions quickly, trust the dashboard, understand their current cashflow period, and act on practical budgeting advice with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Premium, trustworthy, practical. The interface should feel calm and well-made, with enough polish to inspire confidence but not so much decoration that it distracts from personal finance tasks. The voice should stay clear, helpful, and Indonesian-first.

## Anti-references

This should not look or feel like flashy fintech marketing, crypto dashboards, generic corporate BI dashboards, purple AI-product slop, or colorful decorative pages. Avoid heavy gradients, attention-seeking visual effects, overdesigned data cards, and loud palettes that make the tool feel less private or less trustworthy.

## Design Principles

- Make the next financial action obvious: every screen should help users check, add, correct, or decide something concrete.
- Earn trust through restraint: use polished details, consistent components, and readable hierarchy instead of spectacle.
- Keep personal finance private and calm: avoid loud colors, gamification, or dashboard theatrics that amplify anxiety.
- Prefer practical Indonesian clarity: labels, states, and guidance should be understandable without financial jargon.
- Surface insight in context: summaries, budgets, and advisor responses should connect directly to the user's current cashflow period and recorded data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA by default. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, readable Indonesian copy, and color-blind-safe status communication that does not rely on color alone. Body text and placeholders should meet contrast requirements, and touch targets should remain comfortable on mobile Telegram WebView screens.
